# Mochi

From [Mochi](https://github.com/miflow13/mochi-desktop) at `471342631206c40d56bbba016578926e16ca8d0b` (`assets/mochi`), by the Mochi contributors. Its README says "Mochi is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE)", and its Artist Kit says "Mochi is released under the repository's [MIT License](../LICENSE). By submitting artwork for inclusion in Mochi, make sure you have the right to contribute it under the project's license." The clips are those frames played in sequence at the frame rates of `assets/mochi/manifest.json`, the 64-pixel sheets scaled to the 256-pixel canvas as Mochi does, cropped by `tools/framepet` from `tools/framepet/pets/mochi.json`.
