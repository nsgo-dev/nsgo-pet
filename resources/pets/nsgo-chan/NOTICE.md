# NSGo 酱

NSGo-chan, nsgo-pet's official character, and her Gopher are nsgo-dev's own characters, drawn for nsgo-pet from the character sheets in [docs/nsgo-chan](../../../docs/nsgo-chan). Each clip's key poses were drawn with GPT-Image-2 from those sheets, and the clips composed from them as 512×512 PNG frames at 24 fps.

The Gopher is modeled on the Go gopher, designed by Renee French and licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
