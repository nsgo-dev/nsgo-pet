# 大蓝鱼 (Big Blue Fish)

The character is the community's DeepSeek "大肥鱼" (a whale girl), not an official DeepSeek character. Its original, 溟月, is by [@上善无形](https://space.bilibili.com/4456176), and the DeepSeek maid version is designed by [@ZipZipPipe](https://space.bilibili.com/4168597).

The 106 clips come from [PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet](https://github.com/PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet), by way of [Bai-Cute/big-blue-fish-desktop-pet](https://github.com/Bai-Cute/big-blue-fish-desktop-pet) at `75cd217568e713a7de8812f65dad7072798a8b70` (`code/BigBlueFish.Windows/assets/fish`, its 640x360 `compat` set), which also wrote `animations.json`. The `mov/` files are those clips transcoded to HEVC with alpha.
